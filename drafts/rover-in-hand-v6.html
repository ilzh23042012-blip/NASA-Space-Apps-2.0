Rover in Hand

An abandoned rover, not forgotten. Hold it in one hand and scan it with the other. When you find a fault, the view zooms into the repair bay, where you fix that one part with your hands: undo bolts, swap parts, reconnect cables, wipe off dust.

  • Holding hand (left on screen): carries the rover. Tilt and twist to turn it, spread your fingers to open it up.
  • Tool hand (right on screen): point at a part to scan it. Keep pointing at a faulty part to open the repair bay.
  • In the repair bay both hands are tools: pinch and twist to turn bolts, pinch and drag to move parts and plugs, open hand to wipe.
  • Hold still to lock the rover so both hands can work. V-sign: thermal view · Fist: hint · Thumbs-up: next level

About

Made for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge: Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.

Each transmission tells a rover's real history, from NASA sources. Launching an expedition takes you to that rover in 2075: a fictional mission where you fly there, walk up to it, act out what really went wrong in a hands-on repair bay (webcam hand tracking or mouse), and drive it one last time. Fiction and real history are labelled throughout.

Sources: NASA History Office (Apollo 15 and 17 mission summaries), NASA Science: Spirit and Opportunity. 3D models are simplified illustrations built in code.

Abandoned, Not Forgotten

Four spacecraft that NASA lost or left behind: Opportunity, Spirit, Voyager and the Viking 1 lander. Each one has the faults it really had. Pick a mission, scan the craft with your hands, and fix each fault in the repair bay.

  • Holding hand (left on screen): carries the craft. Tilt and twist to turn it, spread your fingers to open it up.
  • Tool hand (right on screen): point at a part to scan it. Keep pointing at a faulty part to open the repair bay.
  • In the repair bay both hands are tools: pinch and twist to turn bolts and valves, pinch and drag to move parts, plugs and memory blocks, open hand to wipe. Strings stretch between your fingertips like a cat's cradle: some repairs use them as a tow line.
  • Hold still to lock the craft so both hands can work. V-sign: thermal view · Fist: hint · Thumbs-up: next mission

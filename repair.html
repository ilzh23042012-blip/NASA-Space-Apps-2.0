Abandoned, Not Forgotten: Rover Rescue

Four NASA rovers were left behind on the Moon and Mars. Use your own hands, tracked by your webcam, to inspect each one, act out what really happened to it, and learn why it still matters.

  • Point to scan · Pinch to grab · Open your fingers to drop · Twist to turn screws and knobs · Open palm to wipe
  • Glowing cat's cradle strings stretch between your fingertips: some tasks use them as tape or a tow line.
  • One hand holds and turns the rover, the other scans. Hold still to lock it so both hands are free.

The camera needs your permission and a secure page (https:// or localhost). Video is processed only in your browser and is never uploaded.
